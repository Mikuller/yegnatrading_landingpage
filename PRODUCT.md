## Platform

web

## Users

Commercial contractors, real estate developers, structural engineers, government and public enterprise procurement officers, and residential builders across Ethiopia who need reliable, certified construction materials delivered on schedule without counterfeit risks or delivery bottlenecks.

## Product Purpose

Provide Ethiopian builders with transparent access to certified construction supplies—primarily Grade 75 steel rebar, Portland cement (OPC/PPC), 6mm staffa steel coil, and formwork plywood—backed by transparent weight metrics, verifiable mill batch certifications, and rapid centralized logistics dispatch.

## Positioning

"ንግድ በእምነትና በታማኝነት!" ("Trade with trust and integrity!"). Yegna Trading bridges the gap between mill production and active job sites by offering authenticated Grade 75 rebar across all standard metric diameters (8mm–32mm) alongside direct mill pricing, guaranteed weight calculations, and established physical headquarters in Kazanchis, Addis Ababa with nationwide site delivery.

## Operating Context

Job sites operate under tight structural pour schedules, fluctuating currency/material pricing, and stringent municipal quality compliance in Ethiopia. Buyers navigate purchasing via direct phone hotlines, Telegram channels, on-site depot visits, and official RFQs requiring VAT fiscal invoices and mill test certificates.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Full metric rebar conversion specs (6mm to 32mm with pcs-per-kg, diameter, and structural application mapping).
- Bilingual Amharic and English localization with instant language switching across all pages and labels.
- Dual contact and quotation channels: instant online quotation builder with structural prefill, direct phone desks (Sales, Customer Service, Order, Logistics), Telegram broadcast/chat, and Crisp help desk.
- Strict constraint: Grade 75 yield strength specifications and Ethiopian/international compliance standards must remain accurate and verifiable; pricing and contact details reflect current Addis Ababa operations.

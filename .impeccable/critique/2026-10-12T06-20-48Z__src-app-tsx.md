### Design Specificity Verdict

**Verdict**: High domain authenticity in physical operations, severely wounded by civil engineering conversion errors and generic SaaS aesthetic tropes.

- **LLM Assessment**: The application captures genuine Ethiopian construction commerce: Grade 75 metric diameters (8mm–32mm), Amharic/English bilingual parity, 7 physical depots spanning Addis Ababa corridors to Bahirdar, and a custom structural visualizer (`RebarLab`). However, its authority with engineers is undermined by inverting weight units to "75.72 Pcs/Kg" for 32mm rebar, naming conflicting executives (Ato Zelalem Alene vs Temesgen Alemayehu), promising a slow 24–48hr quote SLA, and decorating an industrial materials website with Web3/SaaS tropes (mouse cursor glow, tilted marquee, hairline grid backgrounds).
- **Deterministic Scan**: The detector flagged 2 rules across `src/style.css`:
  - `gradient-text` (`src/style.css:132`): Warning. In light mode, amber gradient text drops contrast below 2.5:1.
  - `codex-grid-background` (`src/style.css:103`): Advisory. The hairline grid background is justifiable in RebarLab's blueprint diagram, but has been stamped onto 6 unrelated marketing cards (`Hero`, `Branches`, `Quote`, `About`).
- **Visual Overlays**: Live browser injection not run this session (static CLI mode); deterministic AST and code inspection findings cited directly.

### Overall Impression

The core architecture, Ethiopian geographic specificity, and `RebarLab` load-path visualizer demonstrate outstanding domain awareness. But credibility in structural engineering requires zero tolerance for unit errors, accessible contrast, and industrial gravitas over cosmetic web effects.

---

### What's Working

1. **RebarLab Load-Path to Quote Bridge**: Allowing engineers and contractors to inspect a footing or column and instantly inject verified diameters into the quotation builder is world-class contextual commerce.
2. **True Local Operational Footprint**: Full geographic and contact transparency across 7 verified branch depots with exact coordinates, dedicated phone desks, and official company verification documents.
3. **High-Velocity Mobile Ergonomics**: Persistent sticky mobile bar (`Call Now`, `Get Quote`, `Telegram`) directly serving site superintendents on noisy, sunlit jobsites.

### Priority Issues

- **[P0] Inverted Rebar Conversion Units & Engineering Inaccuracy**
  - **Why it matters**: Labelling 12m bar weight as "75.72 Pcs/Kg" asserts 1kg contains 75 bars of 32mm steel. Consulting engineers and quantity surveyors will immediately dismiss the platform.
  - **Fix**: Change table header to `Weight per 12m Bar (Kg)` and label values as `Kg/pc` (or `6.31 Kg/m`).
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable clarify src/data.ts src/sections/Products.tsx`

- **[P1] Executive Identity Mismatch**
  - **Why it matters**: `Ceo.tsx` quotes "Temesgen Alemayehu", while `PRODUCT.md` and `data.ts` establish "Ato Zelalem Alene". Discrepancies in leadership violate the company's core promise of business integrity.
  - **Fix**: Synchronize leadership identity across `Ceo.tsx`, `Team.tsx`, and `PRODUCT.md` under General Manager Ato Zelalem Alene.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable clarify src/sections/Ceo.tsx`

- **[P1] Lethargic Quote Response SLA**
  - **Why it matters**: A "24–48 business hours" response time triggers operational dread for site contractors facing imminent pour schedules and fluctuating steel prices.
  - **Fix**: Update copy to "Quotes dispatched within 1–2 hours during business hours; call directly for immediate truck dispatch."
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable clarify src/sections/Quote.tsx`

- **[P2] SaaS Gimmicks & Visual Noise**
  - **Why it matters**: Infinite full-screen `RebarBackdrop` drift, traveling sheen, desktop `CursorGlow`, and tilted marquee drain mobile batteries and make a serious industrial supplier look like a frivolous tech template.
  - **Fix**: Level the marquee, remove cursor tracking glows, and restrain background animation to an opt-in or static industrial texture.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable distill src/App.tsx src/style.css`

- **[P2] Monolithic 12-Section Page Length**
  - **Why it matters**: Over 10,000px of continuous vertical scroll exhausts users before they reach conversion tools.
  - **Fix**: Cluster content into clear tabs/anchors (Materials & Specs, Depots & Delivery, Corporate & Careers).
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable layout src/App.tsx`
